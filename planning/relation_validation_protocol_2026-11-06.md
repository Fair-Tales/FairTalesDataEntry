## 2. Crosswalk: the binding constraint is alignment

Preserve immutable source/reference records, raw predictions, flags and their hashes. Build a crosswalk with: historical book/title/edition → canonical work and edition; old `validation_sample_id`/section ID or sentence SID; original text and source offsets; source page/image hashes and evidence region; current OCR revision/hash and offsets; current prediction IDs; alignment class; method/version; reviewer; and unresolved reason. Stable source identity, not numerical ID coincidence or model agreement, determines a join.

Use these classes, frozen before scoring:

1. **Exact source match:** same edition and unique utterance in verified source context, with identical text after the declared reversible whitespace/line-break normalisation. Mechanical uniqueness is insufficient if edition or source location is unknown. Preserve the literal strings and normalisation map.
2. **Source-verified equivalent span:** punctuation/OCR changes or a split/merge preserve the same complete spoken material, surrounding attribution evidence and endpoint assignment. A reviewer resolves the source correspondence without seeing either system's proposed identities. Multiple new fragments mapping to one old section remain **one old scoring unit**; all its required fragments must be recovered and attributed correctly. A merged section crossing different endpoint assignments is not a transferable single-answer label.
3. **Casualty requiring source work:** missing/changed material, repeated text without unique context, changed edition, endpoint-changing boundaries, or unavailable source evidence. Do not transfer the old accepted answer. Keep a reason and the old unit in the alignment denominator. Recover source truth or report it as unresolved with best/worst-case bounds.

The source-first rule also prevents an extraction miss being disguised as alignment loss: once the old source span is securely anchored in the same photo book, absence from new OCR/output is a **pipeline miss**, not a reason to exclude it. A new unmatched emitted section is separately an unsupported new output requiring source adjudication if its accuracy is to be claimed. Neither old false positives nor hard recipients may be selectively dropped.

Local feasibility is measurable, but photo alignment is not yet measured. Against surviving `book_dataframe.json`, 131/200 historical section strings match exactly and 12 after whitespace normalisation. Removing the unjudged final section gives **130 exact + 12 normalised = 142/199 (71.36%)**, with 57 requiring more work. This is alignment to surviving historical text, not current photo OCR; it neither guarantees 142 photo matches nor proves the other 57 are unusable. Use **about 140 reusable sections as a provisional staffing scenario**, with sensitivity at 120/160/199. Replace it with an actual crosswalk census before scheduling human work. Report survival separately for accepted/rejected, raw/matched, ambiguity and OCR-quality strata: a high overall match rate can hide selective loss.

For detection, reconstruct the original sentence sequence and verify book, sentence text and source location before assigning the 650 labels. `all_sentences_oldenv.tsv` is only three bytes and is not a usable crosswalk. The 233 numerical SID intersections with the workbook do not establish a semantic join. Once source anchored, the old human binary answer can score speech detection on that sentence even if current segmentation differs. Freeze how fragments map to sentence-level “contains direct speech”; this binary task does not measure recovery of every word or every utterance inside a multi-utterance sentence. Current precision uses current positives in the source-labelled frame, not the historical 263 positives. Source-only passages remain in the denominator even if the new model emits nothing.

**No fresh detection annotation is required for the aligned 650-label historical benchmark.** Unrecoverable sentence identity limits usable alignment and requires crosswalk/source recovery, not relabelling every sentence. A new, untouched speech-detection claim on the separate human-entered cohort is a different estimand: human page transcription alone does not provide its binary speech labels. Do not imply that recycling the 650 proves performance on that cohort.

## 3. Efficient correction with preserved blinding and provenance

The [audit interface design](audit_interface_design_2026-11-06.md) is the implementation specification. Use a dedicated audit workflow linked from the existing Validation page; its blind label store and lock boundary must be distinct from value-seeded editors that expose and write through current results. The chapter's bespoke interface provides the useful precedent: already-selected passage, adjacent context, visible outputs and compact repeatable decisions made review easy. Carry forward the preloaded source, short decision path, context expansion and automatic advance. Retain visible agent answers only for the explicitly exposed Run queue, not independent truth collection.

The correction task is small: identify who spoke and who was addressed on the 17 unresolved sections and any genuine alignment casualties. Put the page image and source-anchored speech span on screen before the annotator arrives. Use stable neutral character IDs from the independently human-coded source inventory, not a prediction-ranked shortlist. Support add-entity, narrator, collective, no recipient, multiple/admissible recipients and unresolved source. A source-context option must remain available when the inventory is incomplete.

One key selects an endpoint answer; one speaker decision plus one recipient decision needs two keys in the ordinary case. **An explicit lock/advance action makes three keys per two-endpoint section** and is worth retaining to prevent accidental submissions or answer leakage. No prefilled answers, no default-correct checkboxes, no model confidence, provider identity, historical correctness flags or proposed identities appear during the blind pass. Complex ambiguity requires more work rather than being forced into a one-key incorrect label. Do not auto-select the AI's best answer to achieve the keystroke target.

The order is:

1. Freeze the source frame, crosswalk rules, codebook, output schema, selection probabilities and exposure register. Tune and choose the release policy on the imported 161; seal proof membership and the separate historical benchmark role.
2. Freeze predictions, failures, abstentions and hashes for every evaluated provider/policy. Diagnostic human-text/human-inventory arms are separately labelled; the autonomous arm uses its own OCR and inventory. Extractors do not see the hidden human answers or the other provider's output.
3. The human corrector independently labels the unresolved source endpoint(s), with original context, before seeing any AI answer. Queue selection may depend on old errors or discrepancies; hide why an item was selected and mix queue sources. Selection by disagreement does not itself make an answer unblinded, but disclosing the error direction would.
4. Save and **lock immutable initial labels** before revealing outputs. Record literal evidence, normalised identity/set, direct/indirect status where relevant, uncertainty, source regions, annotator, codebook version and timestamp. Preserve accepted historical labels in their original provenance tier.
5. Only then compare old/new outputs, derive error flags and adjudicate genuine source/codebook issues. A second human independently reviews unresolved or direction-changing cases before seeing initial labels; Chris or a designated third adjudicator resolves remaining disagreement against the source. Keep all initial answers and reasons; no silent overwrite to agree with a model. Any proof-informed tuning consumes that proof material.

Run-phase review is deliberately different: the agent's recommendation, concise reason and source evidence arrive together, and a human confirms, rejects/corrects or defers with one common-path key. These records are permanently marked **prediction-exposed operational review** and cannot become independent proof truth. An independently selected blind audit sample must be assigned before a reviewer sees recommendations. Streamlining and blinding coexist through preloading, stable choices and fewer navigation steps; the genuine conflict is answer exposure, where rigour wins.

Agent work can prepare manifests, source crops, crosswalk candidates, queues, scoring and reports. Model annotations are assistance, not independent human truth for either provider. Cross-provider agreement does not upgrade provenance. The former 50-human/150-model/200-section staging and proxy-label certification are retired: they buy no necessary corrected answer that the 17-case pass plus alignment does not provide.

## 4. Human workload conditional on alignment

The annotation starting point is **17 sections, not 50 or 200**. Let `R` be the 17 rejected sections, `U` the old sections whose endpoints cannot be reused after alignment but have recoverable source evidence, and `X` any additional new source sections chosen for scoring. The corrected-section count is **`K = |R ∪ U ∪ X|`**, not `17 + |U| + |X|` without removing overlaps. Old source sections with no usable image remain unresolved; labelling cannot manufacture their truth. The optional unjudged 200th section adds one if included. Only failed endpoints need new truth for scoring. To hide which endpoint failed, the blind correction screen asks both questions with neither prefilled; this adds at most one simple judgement per section without invalidating the accepted counterpart's historical provenance. Budget two decisions per displayed section, as below.

If `A` of the 199 old sections retain reusable correspondence, an intentionally conservative staffing upper bound is `min(199, 17 + 199 − A)` old-section judgements; this assumes every casualty is recoverable and needs a new answer, and subtracts no overlap with the 17. It is an upper planning envelope, not a requirement to label known accepted answers again.

| Photo alignment scenario, not an observed result | Old sections needing corrected answers, before overlap reduction | Correction time at 45–90 seconds/section |
|---|---:|---:|
| All 199 reusable | 17 | 13–26 minutes |
| 160 reusable | At most 56 | 42–84 minutes |
| About 140 reusable | At most 76 | 57–114 minutes |
| 120 reusable | At most 96 | 72–144 minutes |

These times include reading the preloaded span and making both endpoint decisions; they are design assumptions to be replaced by measured interface timing. Keystrokes are not a substitute for reading. Budget separately: approximately 30 minutes for codebook/onboarding; `E × 20–40 seconds` for `E` alignment candidates needing contextual human review; additional source retrieval/edition reconstruction; and second-person adjudication of unresolved cases. Exact source-proven joins can be generated mechanically with logged quality checks. Reviewing all 199 contextual joins would add approximately 66–133 minutes; it is not 199 new attribution annotations. Detection's 650-label crosswalk has a separate alignment-time ledger, **zero planned new detection labels** for the reusable benchmark.

Thus a well-aligned historical attribution correction can take about **43–56 minutes plus alignment and exceptional adjudication**, rather than a wholesale annotation programme. In the 140-match scenario, the corresponding allowance is roughly **87–144 minutes plus alignment and exceptional adjudication**. Publish actual items, endpoint decisions, keystrokes, active time, alignment failures and unresolved truth. Any extra source-labelled proof sections in the separate 57 books are an additional task and must never be hidden inside this 17-case budget.

The formula `0.05^(1/n)` is the one-sided 95% **zero-error capacity**, not an observed lower bound before a run and not a transfer guarantee. At its exact ceiling the corresponding accepted-output design permits no errors inside accepted field/book outputs. Whole field/book abstentions count against coverage and all-arrival recovery, not as correct outputs; omissions inside an accepted output cannot be relabelled abstentions. Accepted-only accuracy and all-arrival recovery are separate estimands. Do not round a threshold upward, lower it after seeing failures, or report an error-containing result at the perfect-result bound. All-perfect accepted-book success is defined prospectively per field; it cannot be substituted afterwards for a more forgiving item score. A finite census has an exact finite-frame score, not automatically a confidence interval for future books. This design reports pointwise 95% bounds, with every tested endpoint disclosed; these are not a joint 95% pipeline certificate. A future joint claim would require prespecified simultaneous-error control and recalculated capacities.

## 6. Directional adjudication and honest human comparison

The owner's completeness hypothesis is credible but must remain falsifiable. The dev20 adjudications resolved seven reference-empty disagreements in the AI's favour with zero hallucinations. All seven recovered `skipped_wordless` pages nevertheless had `continuity.confidence="high"`, `flows_continuously=true`, `text_appears_missing=false` and fluent narrative justifications: **the continuity judge was confidently wrong 7 for 7**. Low reference alias precision (0.447) is consistent with incomplete human lists; it is not proof that every new AI alias or attribution is correct.

Use the main design's probability-sampled, source-blind directional audit. Census rare AI-only/reference-only/identity-conflict cases when feasible, retain the exact stratum sampling probabilities and inflation factors, and include probability-sampled agreement/source-negative controls so shared omissions remain detectable. A correction queue chosen because an old flag is wrong is useful for repairing labels; it is **not** a representative estimate of new AI-versus-human disagreement directions. Keep those designs distinct.

For an attribution disagreement, the blind human first identifies the source-supported speaker/recipient/set without seeing either candidate or which side is the reference. A second blind source judgement resolves disputed directions; reveal predictions only after initial labels lock. The adjudicated outcomes are AI correct/reference wrong, reference correct/AI wrong, both wrong, both admissible and unresolved. Report the weighted share favouring AI among the specified disagreement population, with an interval and unresolved sensitivity. Do not force every disagreement into one winner.

Report **reference capture** (recovery of eligible existing human labels), **adjudicated source completeness** (recovery of the enlarged source-supported opportunity set), and **source correctness** separately. A human omission belongs in the enlarged truth denominator once; it is not also a pipeline miss if AI recovered it. AI-only candidates must be source verified before increasing truth or the numerator. Reference-only candidates found unsupported are reference errors, not AI misses. Estimated omitted truth from weighted sampling carries uncertainty; it is not silently treated as a fully enumerated gold corpus. Items missed by both systems require source-negative audits to discover them.

The defensible publication framing is **non-inferiority in adjudicated source completeness, plus measured directional superiority where supported**, not agreement with an incomplete reference or presumed AI superiority. Non-inferiority requires a prespecified substantively acceptable margin and an interval for the paired completeness difference; superiority among disagreements alone does not establish overall superiority or non-inferiority. The main design proposes a **1-percentage-point margin for source recovery of text, characters and aliases**, subject to Chris's ratification and the separate absolute quality gates. Do not transplant it to raw speaker/recipient accuracy without a task-specific justification. If no margin is ratified or the interval fails, report estimated differences without the non-inferiority claim. A paper can state that the frozen system recovered at least as much source-supported information within the declared margin on the eligible human-entered cohort, and that a measured weighted fraction of disagreements were human omissions. It must disclose the 57-book population, heterogeneity, incomplete labels, source audit, exposure and historical benchmark role. It cannot claim a universal human ceiling, independent IAA or unrestricted future-book autonomy.
