## 2. Crosswalk: the binding constraint is alignment

Preserve immutable source/reference records, raw predictions, flags and their hashes. Build a crosswalk with: historical book/title/edition → canonical work and edition; old `validation_sample_id`/section ID or sentence SID; original text and source offsets; source page/image hashes and evidence region; current OCR revision/hash and offsets; current prediction IDs; alignment class; method/version; reviewer; and unresolved reason. Stable source identity, not numerical ID coincidence or model agreement, determines a join.

Use these classes, frozen before scoring:

1. **Exact source match:** same edition and unique utterance in verified source context, with identical text after the declared reversible whitespace/line-break normalisation. Mechanical uniqueness is insufficient if edition or source location is unknown. Preserve the literal strings and normalisation map.
2. **Source-verified equivalent span:** punctuation/OCR changes or a split/merge preserve the same complete spoken material, surrounding attribution evidence and endpoint assignment. A reviewer resolves the source correspondence without seeing either system's proposed identities. Multiple new fragments mapping to one old section remain **one old scoring unit**; all its required fragments must be recovered and attributed correctly. A merged section crossing different endpoint assignments is not a transferable single-answer label.
3. **Casualty requiring source work:** missing/changed material, repeated text without unique context, changed edition, endpoint-changing boundaries, or unavailable source evidence. Do not transfer the old accepted answer. Keep a reason and the old unit in the alignment denominator. Recover source truth or report it as unresolved with best/worst-case bounds.

The source-first rule also prevents an extraction miss being disguised as alignment loss: once the old source span is securely anchored in the same photo book, absence from new OCR/output is a **pipeline miss**, not a reason to exclude it. A new unmatched emitted section is separately an unsupported new output requiring source adjudication if its accuracy is to be claimed. Neither old false positives nor hard recipients may be selectively dropped.

Local feasibility is measurable, but photo alignment is not yet measured. Against surviving `book_dataframe.json`, 131/200 historical section strings match exactly and 12 after whitespace normalisation. Removing the unjudged final section gives **130 exact + 12 normalised = 142/199 (71.36%)**, with 57 requiring more work. This is alignment to surviving historical text, not current photo OCR; it neither guarantees 142 photo matches nor proves the other 57 are unusable. Use **about 140 reusable sections as a provisional staffing scenario**, with sensitivity at 120/160/199. Replace it with an actual crosswalk census before scheduling human work. Report survival separately for accepted/rejected, raw/matched, ambiguity and OCR-quality strata: a high overall match rate can hide selective loss.

For detection, reconstruct the original sentence sequence and verify book, sentence text and source location before assigning the 650 labels. `all_sentences_oldenv.tsv` is only three bytes and is not a usable crosswalk. The 233 numerical SID intersections with the workbook do not establish a semantic join. Once source anchored, the old human binary answer can score speech detection on that sentence even if current segmentation differs. Freeze how fragments map to sentence-level “contains direct speech”; this binary task does not measure recovery of every word or every utterance inside a multi-utterance sentence. Current precision uses current positives in the source-labelled frame, not the historical 263 positives. Source-only passages remain in the denominator even if the new model emits nothing.

**No fresh detection annotation is required for the aligned 650-label historical benchmark.** Unrecoverable sentence identity limits usable alignment and requires crosswalk/source recovery, not relabelling every sentence. A new, untouched speech-detection claim on the separate human-entered cohort is a different estimand: human page transcription alone does not provide its binary speech labels. ## 4. Human workload conditional on alignment

The annotation starting point is **17 sections, not 50 or 200**. Let `R` be the 17 rejected sections, `U` the old sections whose endpoints cannot be reused after alignment but have recoverable source evidence, and `X` any additional new source sections chosen for scoring. The corrected-section count is **`K = |R ∪ U ∪ X|`**, not `17 + |U| + |X|` without removing overlaps. Old source sections with no usable image remain unresolved; labelling cannot manufacture their truth. The optional unjudged 200th section adds one if included. Only failed endpoints need new truth for scoring. To hide which endpoint failed, the blind correction screen asks both questions with neither prefilled; this adds at most one simple judgement per section without invalidating the accepted counterpart's historical provenance. Budget two decisions per displayed section, as below.

If `A` of the 199 old sections retain reusable correspondence, an intentionally conservative staffing upper bound is `min(199, 17 + 199 − A)` old-section judgements; this assumes every casualty is recoverable and needs a new answer, and subtracts no overlap with the 17.
