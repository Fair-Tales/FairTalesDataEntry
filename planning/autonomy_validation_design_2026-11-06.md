## 2. Field-specific ceilings, reference limits and directional adjudication

### What a ceiling means

Use pointwise one-sided 95% bounds, not a joint 95% pipeline guarantee. For an independent binary unit, the zero-error maximum lower bound is `C(n)=0.05^(1/n)`. For independent work-level bounded scores with frozen normalised weights use `C_f=0.05^w_max,f` and the conservative method in §5. **Set each quality gate to that endpoint's own attainable ceiling, not a flat 0.90.** Quality here concerns accepted field/book outputs. This is a stringent perfect-observed-score gate on those outputs: any error or fractional loss inside an accepted output lowers its bound below that ceiling. It is not a guarantee that the pipeline will pass, and ceiling feasibility is not by itself scientific adequacy. If an endpoint's ceiling is too low for the research use, acquire new truth/works or withhold that claim; never quietly lower its gate.

Pages, aliases and characters share books. The attractive item ceilings below are **independent-item sizing limits**, not justified future-book certificates. Report the finite-corpus item score exactly, alongside work-cluster inference. Claiming 702 independent pages or 1,297 independent characters would be pseudoreplication. This is why all-book endpoints can legitimately share a work ceiling while alias-positive and category-specific endpoints have different denominators. A finite census has no sampling error for its own locked labels, but still has annotation and transport uncertainty.

**Make ceilings compatible with abstention.** Before outcomes, freeze the full eligibility frame, automated acceptance policy and a ceiling lookup/formula for every accepted-count/weight profile allowed by the coverage floors. After the run select the prescribed row using that policy's accepted outputs; never choose an easier rule after seeing truth, select a provider on proof, or retrospectively relabel an erroneous output as abstained. Report accepted/withheld counts and outcomes in every stratum. A whole field/book abstention consumes all its eligible opportunities in the coverage denominator. An omitted character, alias or text span **inside an accepted field/book** is an error, not a post hoc abstention. Compute accepted-only quality on the actual accepted works with its own ceiling, and report its actual attainable floor; fewer accepted works mean a weaker numerical claim even if perfect. All-arrival recovery counts abstentions as misses and is always reported, but is not given a zero-loss quality gate. The separate all-arrival completeness non-inferiority claim can still fail because of abstentions. Thus the 95%/90% operational allowances are real, without treating withheld outputs as correct.

For every reported result use `correct/eligible; observed point estimate; lower bound; method/unit; coverage; scope`. An example, **not an observed result**, is `57/57; 1.0000; L=0.9488005, independent works`. No future proof score has been observed here. Publish abstention-inclusive recovery beside accepted-only correctness; accepted denominators are outcomes: the predeclared ceiling rule, not a chosen numerical threshold after outcomes, governs them. Withhold unconditional claims when truth is unresolved.

### Adjudicated directional measurement: falsifiable and economical

**Estimands.** For each field, distinguish (i) recall of valid recorded human positives, (ii) source-grounded completeness of AI and human reference, (iii) false additions/wrong attachment, and (iv) direction of disagreements. Let source-supported unit set T define completeness, with AI A and human reference H. Estimate `R_A=|A∩T|/|T|`, `R_H=|H∩T|/|T|` and paired `Delta=R_A−R_H`; also report book-macro versions. A source-supported fact missing from both lists is in T and counts against both. The union A∪H alone cannot reveal such double omissions, so union-only adjudication licenses **relative recovery over the observed union**, not total recall.

On each sampled disagreement, blind source labels resolve to **AI right/human wrong (A-win), human right/AI wrong (H-win), both wrong, both defensible/tie, unresolved**. Do not collapse uncertainty into an AI win. Report weighted A-win share among all disagreements, resolved share, A-win share among decisive A/H wins with its own denominator, and net `P(A-win)−P(H-win)`. Conditional superiority among decisive disagreements requires its one-sided 95% lower bound >0.5; net directional superiority requires lower bound >0. Both-wrong and unresolved mass must be prominent; show worst/best-case assignments of unresolved cases. A high directional win rate by itself does not certify completeness, hallucination rate or every book.

**Sampling and inflation.** Seal both providers' final outputs and references first. A coordinator builds disjoint strata by field, discrepancy direction (AI-only, reference-only, conflicting value/attachment), reference provenance and annotator; split safety-critical and rare-category cases in advance. Freeze N_h without opening adjudicated outcomes. If a field has ≤100 disagreements, census them; otherwise sample `n_h=min(N_h,20)` uniformly without replacement in each non-empty stratum using a frozen seed. This deliberately oversamples rare discrepancies; if a stratum is tiny it is a census. Record actual inclusion `pi_h=n_h/N_h`, design weight `1/pi_h`, and any extra targeted cases separately. No replacement of difficult cases.

Independently sample 10% of source pages within each proof work (round up, known page inclusion) for source-only transcription/text-presence and inventory/mention checks, including agreements, empty references and pages with no extracted entities. For book-level identity/inventory completeness, independently draw 10% of eligible works within each annotator stratum (round up) for full source checks: a page sample cannot establish an entire book's character inventory. With the current annotator counts, rounding up selects **13 full-source works** (4 from 33, 2 from 13 and one from each of seven smaller strata), not roughly six; this is 22.8% overall, with different inclusion probabilities by annotator. It is additional human work beyond the 17 relation corrections. These sampling fractions are planning defaults frozen before outputs, not claims of adequate precision. Cases selected by both routes retain both design flags and joint inclusion probability; never count them twice. Ordinary agreement opportunities use their independent selection probability, not the disagreement weight. Relative to a hypothetical exact 10% source control, a censused disagreement is sampled at 10× probability; 20/40 gives 5×. Actual rounding/annotator strata change these factors, including 100% selection for singleton strata. The manifest reports actual inflation for every stratum. A 20-fold change in discrepancy prevalence across fields cannot be ignored by pooling unweighted counts.

Estimate directional shares with stratified inverse-probability weighted totals/ratios and finite-frame design-based intervals; for broader population transfer use paired work-cluster intervals and report contributing works. Census disagreements remove sampling uncertainty for that finite discrepancy frame, not human-label error or future-work uncertainty. Do not report a naive binomial interval on the enriched sample. For a reproducible conservative directional interval, invert the finite-population hypergeometric distribution for each outcome count in each sampled stratum, allocate total alpha 0.05 across those stratum/outcome bounds, and project the simultaneous feasible count set onto the prespecified overall share, decisive-win share and net-win contrast. Enforce that the five outcome counts sum to N_h; censused strata have known finite-frame counts. This provides non-degenerate bounds for sparse/all-win samples without treating oversampled units as iid. Broader work-population intervals are separate. Completeness estimates from random source units use their own weights and clustering; they are separate from disagreement-direction estimates. **These are distinct proof denominators.** Exact recorded-positive recovery can use all eligible accepted works with existing labels. Exact source-completeness work scores exist only for the fully source-audited subset (nominal 13 before further exclusions), not all 57. Freeze that subset's ceiling using its actual design weights; sampled pages do not supply an exact full-book score. For the current stratified 13 design, the largest population weight is 33/(57×4), yielding only 0.648176 for a perfect-score conservative work bound; equal-weight C(13) and C(57) are both inappropriate for that weighted estimand. A finite-cohort design-based interval is a different, explicitly declared inference. This small source check is useful for diagnosis, but does not support a strong future-work completeness certificate; enlarge its prespecified source-truth coverage or withhold that claim. For two-stage/sampled adjudication, use design-based estimates carrying both stages' uncertainty and establish their attainable ceiling under that actual design before review. Never insert an inverse-probability estimate as if it were an observed X_i in the 57-work KL formula. If this design cannot support the desired completeness floor, report the estimate/interval and withhold that certificate; a larger, prospectively reserved source audit is required. More annotation within existing works improves finite-frame truth but not the number of independent works.

**Adjudicators and blinding.** One designated domain-competent human codes each selected source unit without seeing AI/reference values, provenance account, correctness flags, confidence, reason for selection, model/provider or disagreement direction. Use source-only crops/context; relation identity palettes must be independently source-established and independent of tested candidates, with no prefill. For text, alias or inventory-completeness adjudication, withhold the compared human transcription/list as well as AI output: an answer list would itself disclose the reference. Use image-first transcription/open source coding where no independent neutral palette exists, even when this needs more than one key. Mix random agreement controls and disagreements in a shuffled queue. The coordinator can know the trigger; the annotator cannot. Blinding is to output/source-of-answer, not a claim the annotator has never read a familiar book. Record prior familiarity and avoid original entrant as sole adjudicator of their own work where feasible. A second human independently codes all unresolved/ambiguous cases and a frozen 20% random reliability subset of the selected units; a referee resolves disagreements after both initial labels lock. No AI vote creates human truth. Preserve dissent and codebook changes; changes that retune the extractor consume affected proof.

Lock initial labels before revealing any candidate or recommendation. A masked scoring/coordinator pass then compares the locked labels to anonymised predictions; randomise candidate order only after lock. ## 3. Provider comparison and bounded automatic validation

### Extraction and verification answer different questions

**Primary P / blind checker C:** for the existing extraction calibration, Anthropic proposes the output and OpenAI independently extracts from the original evidence, without seeing P, human references or adjudication labels. **Relations are a symmetric comparison:** run current frontier candidates from both providers, compare measured quality/coverage/cost on Tune, and freeze the release candidate and checker roles before Prove. Neither provider is designated primary for relations in advance. Use the same semantic extraction prompt, convention bundle and output schema where supported, with recorded provider-native wrappers. This measures provider difference under a common task; do not assume API or image-encoding identity. Each provider's text-dependent stages use its own OCR for the end-to-end comparison. A shared verified-text diagnostic may isolate reasoning errors, but is labelled diagnostic rather than end-to-end evidence.

Cross-check title/author/publisher, text and reading order, text/story presence, characters, aliases, attachments and all emitted character attributes. Relations require the revised validation protocol; they remain experimental until its human-supported gates pass. Preserve raw surfaces and per-provider identity graphs. Equivalence is field-specific: Unicode/spacing rules for text, documented identity equivalences for metadata, evidence-backed character alignment, and separate lexical matching versus alias identity. Case folding must not hide invented-name errors; possessors must not disappear during identity matching. Convention differences are explicit outcomes, not automatically primary faults.

**Asymmetric verifier V:** the other-provider verifier (OpenAI in the original Anthropic-primary arm) then receives a specific primary claim and the relevant original image(s), with neighbouring pages/full-book context when required. It must return `supported / contradicted / insufficient_evidence`, exact supporting or counterevidence, page/region coordinates and a proposed correction when justified. Examples: “Is this printed name *Nbi* or *Nibi*?”, “Does this phrase refer to this character?”, “Which credited organisation is the imprint?”, “Identify printed regions omitted from this transcription.” Request counterevidence and an omission search, not merely a justification for P. A claim-only checker cannot discover an entity P never proposed; retain an independent page/region and character inventory check.

This is a serious alternative to repeating extraction: checking a disputed glyph against a larger original crop may be easier than generating a complete transcription. It may also anchor on P and repeat its error. **Neither superiority nor independence is assumed.** In Tune, compare C alone, V alone and a bounded hybrid on the same preselected development books, including ordinary agreements and known hard cases. Start with 20 Tune books chosen before predictions, expand only within the fixed Tune set and envelope. Run V across their eligible claims, including C/P agreements, so its shared-error detection is measurable. Synthetic deletions, invented-name substitutions and wrong attachments test mechanics/sensitivity, but never contribute to real-corpus accuracy or FNR estimates.

Recommended candidate to test: blind C on every admitted book; V on every identifier claim and on **all substantive disagreements plus predeclared risk triggers** (unusual names, uncertain glyphs, non-English spans, illustration lettering, implicit attribution, unexplained missing regions). V also checks a reproducible **5% random sample of remaining agreement opportunities**, selected by a stable run/input hash; these checks are automated. Freeze the actual triggers, sampling fraction, resource bounds and final acceptance rule before Prove. Random-check misses do not make the unchecked agreements correct; their residual risk must pass calibration. If V-only performs better, selection is permitted in Tune, but a blind extraction arm remains in the calibration experiment to measure agreement explicitly.

### Bounded resolution without a human

1. Deterministic checks establish schema validity, image/page completeness, source quote/offset validity, referential integrity and approved metadata equivalences. These prove structure, not the semantic truth of model evidence.
2. V examines disputed claims using evidence that can differ from the primary's crop/OCR. An uncorroborated model confidence score never decides acceptance.
3. If needed, permit **one adjudication request** against retained original evidence, with conflicting candidates anonymised/reordered and an explicit insufficient-evidence option. This can be a different prompt/model within either funded provider; it is a third *opinion*, not an independent gold judge. No third paid provider is assumed. Cap corrections at one candidate revision and one recheck; no repeated voting until agreement appears.
4. Accept a correction only through the **specific correction policy proved on held-out truth**, including its wrong-change rate. Otherwise abstain at the affected field and invalidate dependent outputs. Where truth cannot establish that policy, automatic semantic correction is not certified. An ambiguous alias can be retained as an unassigned surface; a disputed identifier cannot create an authoritative join.

The checker does not merge its extractions into canonical records, take majority votes as truth, or fill every gap. Its output is evidence and a validation disposition. The chosen extractor's initial proposal, all alternatives and the final policy decision remain distinct. Cross-family verification is a deployment component; it is never the independent human truth used to certify that component. Evaluate **final accepted risk**, including correct-primary values damaged by checker/resolver actions; a checker that produces more harmful edits than useful repairs fails regardless of disagreement volume.

### What runs and what is scored

For each book, retain original input bytes and order; immutable references and their provenance; P's raw outputs; blind C's raw outputs; V/adjudication outputs when the tested policy invokes them; final accepted/abstained output; and metered attempts. Scoring never sends human truth to either model. First score P and C independently against eligible reference, then their agreement, then V and the final policy. Define the unit per endpoint in advance (book identifier tuple, text-bearing page/span, known character/alias attachment); cluster all uncertainty at book/work level. Report numerator, denominator and reference tier for every metric, not just percentages. Use a fixed reference-positive denominator for recall, including missed outputs. For output-based groundedness show emitted count, empty-output count and recall alongside it.

For reference-evaluable, nontrivial opportunities, let:

- `E` = P is wrong under the frozen field-specific correctness rule; `K` = P is correct.
- `A` = P and C agree under the frozen substantive equivalence rule; `D` = substantive disagreement; `X` = unavailable/failed/incomparable checker outcome. These are disjoint. An unsupported reference is `U`, outside correctness denominators but inside coverage reporting.
- `n(S)` = count of opportunities in event `S` (or the explicitly stated weighted count). Do not silently mix unit types or reference tiers.

| Quantity | Definition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement rate on content | `n(A) / n(A ∪ D)` plus `X` rate over all eligible attempts | Similarity only; report trivial-agreement share separately over all field opportunities. |
| Correctness given agreement | `n(A ∩ K) / n(A)` on known truth | How often agreement predicts verified primary correctness; often called negative predictive value when disagreement is the alarm. |
| Shared-error risk among agreements | `n(A ∩ E) / n(A)` | Residual risk of trusting agreement; complement of the preceding quantity on the same known-truth denominator. Also report `n(A ∩ E) / n(eligible known-truth attempts)`. |
| **False-negative rate of agreement-as-validator** | **`n(A ∩ E) / n(E)`** | Fraction of genuine primary errors missed because the providers agreed. Report `X ∩ E` separately as blocked/unresolved errors. Detection by substantive disagreement is `n(D ∩ E)/n(E)`, not necessarily `1 − FNR` when X exists. |
| Disagreement yield | `n(D ∩ E) / n(D)` | Fraction of alarms finding a real primary error. |
| False-alarm rate | `n(D ∩ K) / n(K)` | Fraction of correct primary outputs that the checker unnecessarily challenges. Also report false alarms per 100 attempted books. |
| Wasted-alarm share | `n(D ∩ K) / n(D)` | Fraction of disagreements consuming resources without finding a primary error. |

Expand disagreements into **P wrong/C right; P right/C wrong; both wrong differently; both defensibly correct under multiple valid answers**. The last case diagnoses comparator/convention noise. Record unknown-truth disagreements separately; don't label them primary errors. In an agreement, shared correctness remains unmeasurable if the reference does not establish it. Both models omitting a reference-known alias is a shared recovery error, even if their emitted alias sets match perfectly.

These describe the selected invoked population, not all books/claims. The all-claim Tune20 comparison supports a development estimate across its eligible claims; a broader estimate from selectively invoked checks requires frozen, nonzero inclusion probabilities and appropriate selection weights. Neither is a substitute for held-out policy evaluation.

For the complete deployed policy, define **`FNR_policy = n(E ∩ final_wrong ∩ accepted)/n(E)`** over **all eligible, known-truth primary errors**, including erroneous agreements that bypass V. Repaired or abstained primary errors are contained; final accepted errors are missed. Separately measure `wrong accepted / all accepted`, errors prevented or repaired per attempt, **correct-primary → wrong-final** changes, abstention and accepted coverage. Define a policy false alarm as any substantive claim that P is erroneous when P is correct, whether later cleared or not; routine scheduled checks that support P are not alarms. Report false-alarm rate over all known-correct P opportunities, plus correct outputs unnecessarily abstained or changed. An error corrected before acceptance is not a final error; an uncorrected error merely waved through by an adjudicator is. Publish book-level “any material error” rates alongside opportunity metrics.

Quantify checker costs: USD and latency per true error detected/corrected; paid checks and escalation dollars spent on `D ∩ K`; correct outputs turned into abstentions; harmful corrections; human minutes actually used, separated into Tune, independent proof annotation and optional Run audits. With zero detected errors, cost per finding is undefined, with total cost still reported. A high false-alarm checker can make autonomy unusable even when it is cautious. ## 5. What proof establishes

Freeze the whole extraction/checking/correction/abstention policy, endpoints, hypotheses, sampling design, provenance, codebook, normalisation, matching, weights, unknown handling and confidence methods before unblinding. No favourable seed, replacement of failed books, or adding observations until a gate passes. If source qualification changes the 57, freeze the revised denominator and ceiling before outcomes. Sampling of disagreements can depend on sealed discrepancies under §2's rule, but never on their eventual adjudicated correctness.

**Per-field release:** the final-output quality floor, completeness non-inferiority where claimed and operational coverage all pass; references establish that exact claim; required regression/source/admission/publication/accounting checks pass. Report unknown-truth sensitivity. Partial release is explicitly partial. A benchmark win or provider agreement is not a substitute for a failed source-supported gate. No blanket 95% confidence applies to all endpoints together; pointwise alpha 0.05 and all tested endpoints are disclosed. Choose model/provider/policy on Tune, not the better-looking proof result.

**Work-level confidence method.** Apply this to fully observed endpoint scores on the actual accepted works; report all-arrival recovery separately. Let X_i in [0,1] be an independent work's prespecified score, w_i fixed normalised weights, q=sum(w_i X_i), and w_max=max(w_i). Solve `KL(q||L)=w_max log(1/0.05)` with 0≤L≤q, using L=0 at q=0. Here `KL(q||L)=q log(q/L)+(1−q)log((1−q)/(1−L))`. The conservative bounded-variable Chernoff construction permits arbitrary within-work dependence. At q=1 its maximum is **L=0.05^w_max**, which is the field's ceiling under the frozen accepted-profile rule. Equal weights over 57 yield 0.9488005; aliases/categories use only their prespecified eligible positive works, and unequal weights lower the best bound. At that ceiling only q=1 among accepted outputs passes; field/book abstentions count against coverage and all-arrival recovery. Full-source sample weights/denominators and uncertainty are not interchangeable with the 57-reference-work scores. Never round the gate upward beyond attainability or use a rounded printed number as the computational threshold.

Independent, representative works or an explicit probability-sampling frame remain assumptions. This convenience cohort is a finite cohort first. Generalisation is conditional on comparable works, coding conventions and the measured annotator mixture; the same annotator across many books does not establish robustness to new annotators. Report annotator-stratified outcomes and sensitivity to the dominant accounts; fixed strata with no observations remain unsupported. Pointwise intervals do not repair selection or unmeasured population shift.

Report finite-corpus item scores exactly and use whole-work stratified resampling for secondary micro ratios, paired completeness differences, kappa and provider differences, preserving sampling weights. These are approximate transfer intervals; few contributing works or sparse errors can make them uninformative. An all-perfect bootstrap is not a valid zero-width population interval. Use the conservative bounded-work boundary bound above, exact design-based finite-frame bounds where the probability design supports them, and no unsupported item-iid substitution. For paired signed differences on [-1,1], map to [0,1] for a conservative boundary bound; a zero-loss sample alone need not prove a 1-point non-inferiority margin with 57. This is a real power limitation, not permission to assume AI superiority. More source labels on the same works improve within-frame measurement, not the independent-work count.

The supplied ceilings are mathematically correct: C(59)=0.9504924, C(90)=0.9672620, C(161)=0.9815650, C(718)=0.9958364, C(480)=0.9937783, C(1,297)=0.9976929. To establish an independent-unit error upper bound of 1%, 0.5%, 0.1% with zero failures requires 299, 598, 2,995 units respectively; books never become pages or attachments in that arithmetic.
