### Why the imported holdout is valid, and why not pool it

The original study's `character_database.db` supplies genuine human character, alias, gender, human/non-human and protagonist coding. The usable 161 contain **1,297 characters and 178 aliases; 159 books have recorded characters, 90 have aliases, and 71 have no recorded aliases**. This supports independent recovery-of-recorded-human-positives tests if books are withheld before tuning. It does not establish exhaustive source truth. **Plural=False is an import default, not human annotation**; mask anomalous human/non-human codes, retain missingness, and leave unsupported categories uncertified. These qualifications correct the otherwise sound human-fields argument. PDF-layer extraction, model cleaning/OCR and model-derived empty pages remain machine references even on untouched books.

**Do not pool the 57 pilot books and the imported holdout into one inferential denominator.** Their annotators, entry procedures, completeness and provenance differ, with no prespecified common target-population mixture or evidence of transportability. Pilot annotator strata remain visible; imported individual annotators are not recoverable from `pilot_import`. A combined administrative total of 97 protected works is not a 97-work gate. Imported proof buys a separate out-of-tuning test of the original study's human coding and a check on whether pilot results transfer to that entry process. It does not strengthen the pilot ceiling. Require each cohort's own supported quality, coverage and non-inferiority gates for a release claiming both populations; a pass on one cannot compensate for a failure on the other. Restrict any partial release accordingly.

The proposed **40-book holdout is a balance of breadth and tuning material, not a powered ≥95% accuracy design**. Two imported books are valid references but supply almost no useful work-level precision or alias breadth. The illustrative pooled arithmetic C(57+k) is arithmetically correct to the stated rounding, but it does not describe this design and is not quoted as a supported ceiling. ## 2. Field-specific ceilings, reference limits and directional adjudication

### What a ceiling means

Use pointwise one-sided 95% bounds, not a joint 95% pipeline guarantee. For an independent binary unit, the zero-error maximum lower bound is `C(n)=0.05^(1/n)`. For independent work-level bounded scores with frozen normalised weights use `C_f=0.05^w_max,f` and the conservative method in §5. **Set each quality gate to that endpoint's own attainable ceiling, not a flat 0.90.** Quality here concerns accepted field/book outputs. This is a stringent perfect-observed-score gate on those outputs: any error or fractional loss inside an accepted output lowers its bound below that ceiling. It is not a guarantee that the pipeline will pass, and ceiling feasibility is not by itself scientific adequacy. If an endpoint's ceiling is too low for the research use, acquire new truth/works or withhold that claim; never quietly lower its gate.

Pages, aliases and characters share books. The attractive item ceilings below are **independent-item sizing limits**, not justified future-book certificates. Report the finite-corpus item score exactly, alongside work-cluster inference. Claiming 702 independent pages or 1,297 independent characters would be pseudoreplication. This is why all-book endpoints can legitimately share a work ceiling while alias-positive and category-specific endpoints have different denominators. A finite census has no sampling error for its own locked labels, but still has annotation and transport uncertainty.

**Make ceilings compatible with abstention.** Before outcomes, freeze the full eligibility frame, automated acceptance policy and a ceiling lookup/formula for every accepted-count/weight profile allowed by the coverage floors. After the run select the prescribed row using that policy's accepted outputs; never choose an easier rule after seeing truth, select a provider on proof, or retrospectively relabel an erroneous output as abstained. Report accepted/withheld counts and outcomes in every stratum. A whole field/book abstention consumes all its eligible opportunities in the coverage denominator. An omitted character, alias or text span **inside an accepted field/book** is an error, not a post hoc abstention. Compute accepted-only quality on the actual accepted works with its own ceiling, and report its actual attainable floor; fewer accepted works mean a weaker numerical claim even if perfect. All-arrival recovery counts abstentions as misses and is always reported, but is not given a zero-loss quality gate. The separate all-arrival completeness non-inferiority claim can still fail because of abstentions. Thus the 95%/90% operational allowances are real, without treating withheld outputs as correct.

**Operational coverage remains at least 95% of eligible field opportunities and at least 90% of required books, applied separately within each claimed cohort.** Freeze eligible opportunity/book definitions and the acceptance policy before outputs; failures and abstentions stay in the denominators. Report each annotator/reference stratum as well. Neither higher coverage nor accuracy in the other cohort compensates for a failed floor.

For every reported result use `cohort; correct/eligible; observed point estimate; lower bound; method/unit; coverage; scope`. Examples, **not observed results**, are `pilot: 57/57; 1.0000; L=0.9488005, equal independent works` and `imported: 40/40; weighted point 1.0000; L=0.9233230, complement-frame work weights`. No proof score has been observed here. Publish abstention-inclusive recovery beside accepted-only correctness; the frozen acceptance policy selects the applicable count/weight ceiling, never a threshold chosen after outcomes. Withhold claims when truth is unresolved.

No blanket 95% confidence applies to all endpoints together; pointwise alpha 0.05 and all tested endpoints are disclosed. Choose model/provider/policy on Tune, not the better-looking proof result.

**Work-level confidence method.** Apply this to fully observed endpoint scores on the actual accepted works; report all-arrival recovery separately. Let X_i in [0,1] be an independent work's prespecified score, w_i fixed normalised weights, q=sum(w_i X_i), and w_max=max(w_i). Solve `KL(q||L)=w_max log(1/0.05)` with 0≤L≤q, using L=0 at q=0. Here `KL(q||L)=q log(q/L)+(1−q)log((1−q)/(1−L))`. The conservative bounded-variable Chernoff construction permits arbitrary within-work dependence. At q=1 its maximum is **L=0.05^w_max**, which is the field's ceiling under the frozen accepted-profile rule. Equal weights over pilot 57 yield 0.9488005. Imported all-work complement-frame weights yield 0.9233230 at 40 accepted works; the separate equally weighted 40 sizing ceiling is 0.9278425. Imported alias recall uses 22 positive works at most, hence 0.8726946. Character/attribute/category gates use their own eligible positive counts and frozen weights, never nominal 57+40. At that ceiling only q=1 among accepted outputs passes; field/book abstentions count against coverage and all-arrival recovery. Full-source sample weights/denominators and uncertainty are not interchangeable with the full qualified-reference-work scores. Never round the gate upward beyond attainability or use a rounded printed number as the computational threshold.

Independent, representative works or an explicit probability-sampling frame remain assumptions. This convenience cohort is a finite cohort first. Generalisation is conditional on comparable works, coding conventions and the measured annotator mixture; the same annotator across many books does not establish robustness to new annotators. Report annotator-stratified outcomes and sensitivity to the dominant accounts; fixed strata with no observations remain unsupported. Pointwise intervals do not repair selection or unmeasured population shift.

Report finite-corpus item scores exactly and use whole-work stratified resampling for secondary micro ratios, paired completeness differences, kappa and provider differences, preserving sampling weights. These are approximate transfer intervals; few contributing works or sparse errors can make them uninformative. An all-perfect bootstrap is not a valid zero-width population interval. Use the conservative bounded-work boundary bound above, exact design-based finite-frame bounds where the probability design supports them, and no unsupported item-iid substitution. For paired signed differences on [-1,1], map to [0,1] for a conservative boundary bound; a zero-loss sample alone need not prove a 1-point non-inferiority margin even with all 57 pilot works or all 40 imported works; assess each cohort separately. This is a real power limitation, not permission to assume AI superiority. More source labels on the same works improve within-frame measurement, not the independent-work count.
