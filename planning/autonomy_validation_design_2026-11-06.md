#### 1D. Exact schemas, seal and chronology

`metadata/holdout_manifest.json` uses the following **closed schema**: reject unspecified keys and unbounded free-form payloads. All fields are required unless nullable below. Counts are nonnegative integers, dates UTC RFC3339, digests lowercase 64-hex SHA256, probabilities exact integer numerator/denominator objects. No NaN/Infinity. Token lists are sorted and unique; selector input order is recorded separately. Actual implementation/source hashes identify future code, not a claim that it already exists. Every two-stratum count map has exactly `alias_positive` and `alias_zero`; total size is their sum. A sealed `machine/schema_registry.json`, included in `inputs` and the file index, defines the complete JSON types and allowed enums for logical names, category/provenance/origin/source/issue/reason codes and partitions. Its values come from the pinned codebook and capture/exposure policies, never source-derived text. The schema validator rejects unregistered values; no free-text escape field is permitted.

| Field | Exact contents/type |
|---|---|
| `schema_version`, `freeze_id`, `state`, `created_at_utc` | Literals `fairtales-holdout-freeze-v1`, `autonomy-r6-freeze`, `sealed`; timestamp. Drafts have a different filename and cannot authorise Tune. |

## 2. Field-specific ceilings, reference limits and directional adjudication

### What a ceiling means

Use pointwise one-sided 95% bounds, not a joint 95% pipeline guarantee. For an independent binary unit, the zero-error maximum lower bound is `C(n)=0.05^(1/n)`. For independent work-level bounded scores with frozen normalised weights use `C_f=0.05^w_max,f` and the conservative method in §5. **Set each quality gate to that endpoint's own attainable ceiling, not a flat 0.90.** Quality here concerns accepted field/book outputs. This is a stringent perfect-observed-score gate on those outputs: any error or fractional loss inside an accepted output lowers its bound below that ceiling. It is not a guarantee that the pipeline will pass, and ceiling feasibility is not by itself scientific adequacy. If an endpoint's ceiling is too low for the research use, acquire new truth/works or withhold that claim; never quietly lower its gate.

Pages, aliases and characters share books. The attractive item ceilings below are **independent-item sizing limits**, not justified future-book certificates. Report the finite-corpus item score exactly, alongside work-cluster inference. Claiming 702 independent pages or 1,297 independent characters would be pseudoreplication. This is why all-book endpoints can legitimately share a work ceiling while alias-positive and category-specific endpoints have different denominators. A finite census has no sampling error for its own locked labels, but still has annotation and transport uncertainty.

**Make ceilings compatible with abstention.** Before outcomes, freeze the full eligibility frame, automated acceptance policy and a ceiling lookup/formula for every accepted-count/weight profile allowed by the coverage floors. After the run select the prescribed row using that policy's accepted outputs; never choose an easier rule after seeing truth, select a provider on proof, or retrospectively relabel an erroneous output as abstained. Report accepted/withheld counts and outcomes in every stratum. A whole field/book abstention consumes all its eligible opportunities in the coverage denominator. An omitted character, alias or text span **inside an accepted field/book** is an error, not a post hoc abstention. Compute accepted-only quality on the actual accepted works with its own ceiling, and report its actual attainable floor; fewer accepted works mean a weaker numerical claim even if perfect. All-arrival recovery counts abstentions as misses and is always reported, but is not given a zero-loss quality gate. The separate all-arrival completeness non-inferiority claim can still fail because of abstentions. Thus the 95%/90% operational allowances are real, without treating withheld outputs as correct.

**Operational coverage remains at least 95% of eligible field opportunities and at least 90% of required books, applied separately within each claimed cohort.** Freeze eligible opportunity/book definitions and the acceptance policy before outputs; failures and abstentions stay in the denominators. Report each annotator/reference stratum as well. Neither higher coverage nor accuracy in the other cohort compensates for a failed floor.

For every reported result use `cohort; correct/eligible; observed point estimate; lower bound; method/unit; coverage; scope`. Examples, **not observed results**, are `pilot: 57/57; 1.0000; L=0.9488005, equal independent works` and `imported: 80/80; weighted point 1.0000; L=0.9629164, complement-frame work weights`. No proof score has been observed here. Publish abstention-inclusive recovery beside accepted-only correctness; the frozen acceptance policy selects the applicable count/weight ceiling, never a threshold chosen after outcomes. Withhold claims when truth is unresolved.

### Adjudicated directional measurement: falsifiable and economical

**Estimands.** For each field, distinguish (i) recall of valid recorded human positives, (ii) source-grounded completeness of AI and human reference, (iii) false additions/wrong attachment, and (iv) direction of disagreements. Let source-supported unit set T define completeness, with AI A and human reference H. Estimate `R_A=|A∩T|/|T|`, `R_H=|H∩T|/|T|` and paired `Delta=R_A−R_H`; also report book-macro versions. A source-supported fact missing from both lists is in T and counts against both. The union A∪H alone cannot reveal such double omissions, so union-only adjudication licenses **relative recovery over the observed union**, not total recall.

On each sampled disagreement, blind source labels resolve to **AI right/human wrong (A-win), human right/AI wrong (H-win), both wrong, both defensible/tie, unresolved**. Do not collapse uncertainty into an AI win. Report weighted A-win share among all disagreements, resolved share, A-win share among decisive A/H wins with its own denominator, and net `P(A-win)−P(H-win)`. Conditional superiority among decisive disagreements requires its one-sided 95% lower bound >0.5; net directional superiority requires lower bound >0. Both-wrong and unresolved mass must be prominent; show worst/best-case assignments of unresolved cases. A high directional win rate by itself does not certify completeness, hallucination rate or every book.

**Sampling and inflation.** Seal both providers' final outputs and references first. A coordinator builds disjoint strata by field, discrepancy direction (AI-only, reference-only, conflicting value/attachment), reference provenance and annotator; split safety-critical and rare-category cases in advance. If a field has ≤100 disagreements, census them; otherwise sample `n_h=min(N_h,20)` uniformly without replacement in each non-empty stratum using a frozen seed. This deliberately oversamples rare discrepancies; if a stratum is tiny it is a census. Record actual inclusion `pi_h=n_h/N_h`, design weight `1/pi_h`, and any extra targeted cases separately. No replacement of difficult cases.

### What runs and what is scored

For each book, retain original input bytes and order; immutable references and their provenance; P's raw outputs; blind C's raw outputs; V/adjudication outputs when the tested policy invokes them; final accepted/abstained output; and metered attempts. Scoring never sends human truth to either model. Run the primary's real preprocessing, while preserving originals for counterchecks: sharing a mistaken crop with both providers can create shared error. Report shared-preprocessing versus original-image verification explicitly.

First score P and C independently against eligible reference, then their agreement, then V and the final policy. Define the unit per endpoint in advance (book identifier tuple, text-bearing page/span, known character/alias attachment); cluster all uncertainty at book/work level. Report numerator, denominator and reference tier for every metric, not just percentages. Use a fixed reference-positive denominator for recall, including missed outputs. For output-based groundedness show emitted count, empty-output count and recall alongside it.

For reference-evaluable, nontrivial opportunities, let:

- `E` = P is wrong under the frozen field-specific correctness rule; `K` = P is correct.
- `A` = P and C agree under the frozen substantive equivalence rule; `D` = substantive disagreement; `X` = unavailable/failed/incomparable checker outcome. These are disjoint. An unsupported reference is `U`, outside correctness denominators but inside coverage reporting.
- `n(S)` = count of opportunities in event `S` (or the explicitly stated weighted count). Do not silently mix unit types or reference tiers.

| Quantity | Definition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement rate on content | `n(A) / n(A ∪ D)` plus `X` rate over all eligible attempts | Similarity only; report trivial-agreement share separately over all field opportunities. |
| Correctness given agreement | `n(A ∩ K) / n(A)` on known truth | How often agreement predicts verified primary correctness; often called negative predictive value when disagreement is the alarm. |
| Shared-error risk among agreements | `n(A ∩ E) / n(A)` | Residual risk of trusting agreement; complement of the preceding quantity on the same known-truth denominator. Also report `n(A ∩ E) / n(eligible known-truth attempts)`. |
| **False-negative rate of agreement-as-validator** | **`n(A ∩ E) / n(E)`** | Fraction of genuine primary errors missed because the providers agreed. Report `X ∩ E` separately as blocked/unresolved errors. Detection by substantive disagreement is `n(D ∩ E)/n(E)`, not necessarily `1 − FNR` when X exists. |
| Disagreement yield | `n(D ∩ E) / n(D)` | Fraction of alarms finding a real primary error. |
| False-alarm rate | `n(D ∩ K) / n(K)` | Fraction of correct primary outputs that the checker unnecessarily challenges. Also report false alarms per 100 attempted books. |
| Wasted-alarm share | `n(D ∩ K) / n(D)` | Fraction of disagreements consuming resources without finding a primary error. |

Expand disagreements into **P wrong/C right; P right/C wrong; both wrong differently; both defensibly correct under multiple valid answers**. The last case diagnoses comparator/convention noise. Record unknown-truth disagreements separately; don't label them primary errors. In an agreement, shared correctness remains unmeasurable if the reference does not establish it. Both models omitting a reference-known alias is a shared recovery error, even if their emitted alias sets match perfectly.

For V, repeat the table with `pass / flag / abstain`. Let `I` mean V was invoked: **`FNR_V|I = n(I ∩ pass ∩ E)/n(I ∩ E)`**, and residual risk is `n(I ∩ pass ∩ E)/n(I ∩ pass)`. These describe the selected invoked population, not all books/claims. The all-claim Tune20 comparison supports a development estimate across its eligible claims; a broader estimate from selectively invoked checks requires frozen, nonzero inclusion probabilities and appropriate selection weights. Neither is a substitute for held-out policy evaluation.

For the complete deployed policy, define **`FNR_policy = n(E ∩ final_wrong ∩ accepted)/n(E)`** over **all eligible, known-truth primary errors**, including erroneous agreements that bypass V. Repaired or abstained primary errors are contained; final accepted errors are missed. Separately measure `wrong accepted / all accepted`, errors prevented or repaired per attempt, **correct-primary → wrong-final** changes, abstention and accepted coverage. Define a policy false alarm as any substantive claim that P is erroneous when P is correct, whether later cleared or not; routine scheduled checks that support P are not alarms. Report false-alarm rate over all known-correct P opportunities, plus correct outputs unnecessarily abstained or changed. An error corrected before acceptance is not a final error; an uncorrected error merely waved through by an adjudicator is. Publish book-level “any material error” rates alongside opportunity metrics.

Quantify checker costs: USD and latency per true error detected/corrected; paid checks and escalation dollars spent on `D ∩ K`; correct outputs turned into abstentions; harmful corrections; human minutes actually used, separated into Tune, independent proof annotation and optional Run audits. With zero detected errors, cost per finding is undefined, with total cost still reported. A high false-alarm checker can make autonomy unusable even when it is cautious. Compare final risk/coverage/cost against P alone and against blind C; select only from development results.

The calibration report must include all agreements, not only inspected disagreements. Scoring agreements against valid human character/alias/attribute reference supplies a shared-error denominator for those endpoints. Machine-derived imported page reference supplies only discrepancies/agreement; the qualified team cohort has independent human text, with directional source adjudication for its errors/omissions. Relations reuse published truths where aligned and require human answers only where missing or for a separately declared fresh proof frame. Report any available rare-name, non-English, stylised typography, illustration-lettering and implicit-attribution subgroup separately; an absent/unknown subgroup cannot receive a general certificate. No supplied measurement establishes the FNR yet: this experiment measures it where the reference supports truth.

**Required correlated-error strata:** (1) primary-provider-derived reference (`ocr`, cleaned `layer`, `skipped_wordless`), the most direct and potentially largest circularity; (2) shared OCR/preprocessing/crops/page omissions; (3) shared human or generated character inventory and alias table; (4) common task conventions/training errors; (5) same-family extractor/annotator or verifier. Report P/C agreement and apparent reference agreement separately in each available stratum. Empty/empty on `skipped_wordless` is specifically provider agreement with its own earlier decision, not human confirmation. Independent source-image human truth, not another model vote, is what can reveal this channel.

## 5. What proof establishes

**Per-field release:** the final-output quality floor, completeness non-inferiority where claimed and operational coverage all pass; references establish that exact claim; required regression/source/admission/publication/accounting checks pass. Report unknown-truth sensitivity. Partial release is explicitly partial. A benchmark win or provider agreement is not a substitute for a failed source-supported gate. No blanket 95% confidence applies to all endpoints together; pointwise alpha 0.05 and all tested endpoints are disclosed. Choose model/provider/policy on Tune, not the better-looking proof result.

**Work-level confidence method.** Apply this to fully observed endpoint scores on the actual accepted works; report all-arrival recovery separately. Let X_i in [0,1] be an independent work's prespecified score, w_i fixed normalised weights, q=sum(w_i X_i), and w_max=max(w_i). Solve `KL(q||L)=w_max log(1/0.05)` with 0≤L≤q, using L=0 at q=0. Here `KL(q||L)=q log(q/L)+(1−q)log((1−q)/(1−L))`. The conservative bounded-variable Chernoff construction permits arbitrary within-work dependence. At q=1 its maximum is **L=0.05^w_max**, which is the field's ceiling under the frozen accepted-profile rule. Equal weights over pilot 57 yield 0.9488005. Imported all-work complement-frame weights yield 0.9629164 at 80 accepted works; the separate equally weighted 80 sizing ceiling is 0.9632458. Imported alias recall uses 42 positive works at most, hence 0.9311574. Character/attribute/category gates use their own eligible positive counts and frozen weights, never nominal 57+80. At that ceiling only q=1 among accepted outputs passes; field/book abstentions count against coverage and all-arrival recovery. Full-source sample weights/denominators and uncertainty are not interchangeable with the full qualified-reference-work scores. Never round the gate upward beyond attainability or use a rounded printed number as the computational threshold.

Independent, representative works or an explicit probability-sampling frame remain assumptions. This convenience cohort is a finite cohort first. Generalisation is conditional on comparable works, coding conventions and the measured annotator mixture; the same annotator across many books does not establish robustness to new annotators. Report annotator-stratified outcomes and sensitivity to the dominant accounts; fixed strata with no observations remain unsupported. Pointwise intervals do not repair selection or unmeasured population shift.

Report finite-corpus item scores exactly and use whole-work stratified resampling for secondary micro ratios, paired completeness differences, kappa and provider differences, preserving sampling weights. These are approximate transfer intervals; few contributing works or sparse errors can make them uninformative. An all-perfect bootstrap is not a valid zero-width population interval. Use the conservative bounded-work boundary bound above, exact design-based finite-frame bounds where the probability design supports them, and no unsupported item-iid substitution. For paired signed differences on [-1,1], map to [0,1] for a conservative boundary bound; a zero-loss sample alone need not prove a 1-point non-inferiority margin even with all 57 pilot works or all 80 imported works; assess each cohort separately. This is a real power limitation, not permission to assume AI superiority. More source labels on the same works improve within-frame measurement, not the independent-work count.

The old pooled size examples and the bot-inclusive 59/718 do not define current endpoints. Pilot text remains 57/702; imported characters, aliases and attributes use their own selected supported counts. To establish an independent-unit error upper bound of 1%, 0.5%, 0.1% with zero failures requires 299, 598, 2,995 units respectively; books never become pages or attachments in that arithmetic.
