**Recommendation: retain the Samsung stock camera unchanged; use one distributed MacroDroid automation to move new captures into a private book folder only during a bounded book session.** Prepare complete, sanitized sessions locally, then let FolderSync or Autosync copy the dedicated outbox one way into each person's isolated S3 staging prefix, feeding the existing durable batch pipeline. All book photographers are on Android. Self-serve setup, verification on each device and individual credential revocation are release requirements. This is a design recommendation, not a claim that the complete automation already exists or has been qualified.

**The decisive change from round 2 is research validity.** Chris supplies the finding that Samsung restricts third-party Camera2 access: third-party apps lack full sensor data and Samsung processing, often lose ultrawide/macro/front lenses, and commonly produce worse output. The project's measured **OCR F1 of 0.99 came from stock-camera photographs**; close-up focus is especially material to page capture. Switching capture apps changes that measured pipeline's input distribution without a research benefit. Open Camera is therefore **NOT RECOMMENDED**, irrespective of convenient folder segregation. Retaining stock capture avoids that particular change; it does not assert that every Galaxy model or camera setting reproduces 0.99.

The secondary objection is operational: Chris supplies that Open Camera writes outside `DCIM` through Storage Access Framework and that its folder grant can reset, allowing captures to revert to the default location. That can silently bypass sync. The stock Samsung camera cannot be redirected off `DCIM/Camera`; Chris verified that One UI's custom save-folder option applies to screenshots, never camera captures. MacroDroid moves completed stock captures after capture; it does not redirect the camera or change its image processing.

The statement, “I don't want to be manually selecting 200 or more photos, that is the whole point,” remains a **hard disqualifier** for per-batch photo selection. Browser multi-select, Photos Picker and share-sheet selection remain **DISQUALIFIED**, including as interim routes. The recommended recurring effort is **complete a short diagnostic session and confirm its receipt → Start the real book session → shoot with existing black separators → Finish and check counts/status**, with no file picking, albums or manual upload. A Finish action is a deliberate, modest change from round 2's zero-post-shoot aspiration: a timeout cannot prove the last book is complete. Automatic copying remains the target; automatic semantic completion is not assumed. If on-device qualification fails, one bulk date/session move is the allowed fallback; sacrificing the stock-camera evidence is not the fallback. If only strict S3 push compatibility fails, qualify the same capture route via Drive and the shared importer.

**Second workflow: legacy Google Photos sessions are retrieved whole by known dates, through an owner-approved Chrome download into private local staging.** Retain every frame, particularly black separators. Google Photos “book” search and other content-filtered retrieval lose separators and destroy book boundaries; they remain rejected. Takeout remains the larger local-export alternative. Mixed legacy dates require a local admission decision before research upload.

**Privacy is an admission requirement; forward segregation is now temporal.** A folder populated by an armed stock-camera mover is weaker isolation than a separate camera app. Every stock photograph taken while armed can be routed as a book photograph. Neither the folder name, toggle, separator nor a classifier proves subject matter. Expiring authorization and a private local hold before upload reduce this risk; they cannot promise zero personal-photo admission.

1. **Forward source authorization:** enroll one owner/device, source-policy version and fixed outbox. Sync watches only that outbox, never `DCIM/Camera`, the raw book folder, Google Photos or a broad parent. The local capture components receive the minimum qualified access needed to observe new stock captures. Any broader Android grant is disclosed and tested; it does not authorize a camera-roll scan or upload. Default state is OFF, including on import and after restart/reboot unless freshly armed.
2. **Bounded sessions:** Start opens a unique session and limited routing lease. Proposed initial limits are **2 minutes idle, 15 minutes absolute duration, 250 captures including separators, or screen-off**, whichever comes first. These are pilot policy values, not product defaults or a change to the existing 1,200-source batch maximum. Base the ceiling on independently reconciled capture occurrences, not only successful moves; uncertainty holds the session. Use elapsed-time deadlines resilient to wall-clock changes, or hold on a clock discontinuity; never revive an expired lease after reboot. Check capture eligibility/deadlines on each event and again before movement, not just in a timer callback the OS may delay; publication requires separate closure authority below. Hitting a limit disarms and holds the session; it never declares a complete book. Renewal is explicit, never triggered by another photograph. Delayed saves from a valid interval are reconciled privately, not automatically admitted after expiry. Exact support/timing under One UI are **UNVERIFIED**.
3. **Private hold before upload:** stock camera → `DCIM/Camera` → private `Pictures/FairTalesCapture/` → validated/sanitized `Pictures/FairTalesOutbox/` → sync. Paths are proposed, not inspected settings. Only Finish plus complete reconciliation releases an immutable session to the outbox. A known personal shot or gap holds the session locally for exception review; nothing is automatically deleted or silently skipped. Cancelling routing does not cancel writes already published to sync. The incident procedure applies if discovery comes too late.
4. **Legacy or mixed sources:** before any candidate image, thumbnail, raw filename, EXIF, classifier result or source record reaches research S3/Firestore, require private local review and exact ordered-manifest approval. Download, optional classifier and research-admission permissions remain distinct. A date range does not establish book-only content. A forward session with a reported personal interruption becomes a mixed-source exception under this rule.
5. **Metadata without changing capture quality:** retain stock originals privately; sanitize upload copies on-device before the watched outbox, removing GPS and unnecessary device metadata while preserving full-resolution decoded pixels and orientation. Bind original and upload SHA-256 digests; retain required order clues privately. Do not require changing stock-camera settings/JPEG processing or assume MacroDroid/sync sanitizes. Automatic local preparation, metadata handling and atomic publication remain **UNVERIFIED release gates**. No re-encoding change may quietly replace the research input distribution.
6. Haiku remains **OFF, optional and non-load-bearing**. It cannot repair temporal privacy isolation. A personal thumbnail sent to a classifier is already exposure; explicit opt-in remains necessary. No automatic escalation, continuous content scanning or model-based permission to upload. Telemetry uses opaque IDs and aggregate counts, never candidate images, filenames, personal OCR text, keys, signed URLs or free-form model descriptions. Local previews avoid cloud thumbnails and persistent caches.
7. Personal content is prohibited in research storage as a policy, **not guaranteed absent by this automation**. The landing prefix is a book-only transport inbox, not quarantine for discovering personal photos. Nothing automatically deletes phone, Photos, Drive or private legacy originals. Uncertain evidence remains private for resolution; transport-copy lifecycle rules do not authorize original deletion.

**A. Forward workflow — stock capture, one shared automation, local closure.** Do not install a second camera or alter Samsung camera settings. The shared macro controls sessions and moves only completed, identified stock-camera files belonging to a currently authorized session into the private book directory. Use generated session/item IDs and collision-safe storage; preserve original names and capture-time evidence privately. Never treat arrival/move completion order as shooting order. The move retains original bytes in a new local location; it is not a sync deletion rule.

**What the single `.macro` must contain.** Target one exported `FairTalesBookSession-vN.macro`, imported once, with shared event branches/constraints for Start, Photo Taken, Finish, expiry/screen-off and recovery. Include default-OFF state, bounded lease rules, counters, serialized move queue, error holds, visible status, publisher handoff and version information. Packaging all branches/state safely in one export is **UNVERIFIED**: import the exact export on a clean second handset, exercise every branch and inspect a re-export for hidden device state. Import must not retain an active session, previous person's paths, credentials or queued originals. Do not quietly distribute multiple hand-built macros if this fails.

The round-2 **local preparation/publisher remains missing implementation**, not a capability implied by Photo Taken plus Move/Copy. It must supply independent camera-save reconciliation, hashing, metadata-safe copies, closed inventories and atomic outbox publication. First qualify what the one export can express using standard installed apps. If a companion is necessary, it must be one shared, versioned dependency with a written install/verification step and no per-person scripts, shell commands or source edits; the `.macro` remains the sole imported automation. The dependency and maintenance count against the self-serve gate. Until demonstrated, this is a candidate design, not a ready-to-share macro. If setup becomes impractical, use F3 while resolving it.

**Move queue and completed files.** Photo Taken is a discovery hint, not proof that a file is closed or one event equals one photo. Capture exact source identity and eligibility in a durable local journal; deduplicate repeated events by identity/version and serialize bounded work. Independent reconciliation discovers missed events; never use a recursive camera-roll sweep as recovery. Wait for a final decodable file, stable size/mtime across spaced checks and stable digest; repeated reads alone cannot prove a writer will not resume. Qualify actual stock-camera save completion/MediaStore visibility and handle paired/unsupported media explicitly. Never move a file still being written. Verify bytes after movement; failure preserves the source, records a gap and stops publication. Cross-directory rename atomicity, copy-verify-then-remove behavior and Gallery/sync visibility are **UNVERIFIED**. Without a reliable save-completion boundary the mover is not qualified. Publish only finished immutable outbox copies; absent atomic rename, qualify an exclusion/ready protocol that prevents partial sync.

**Completion and completeness are separate from expiry.** Start records a local camera-save baseline/watermark and session ID; Finish defines the intended end of capture and stops new routing. **Capture authority and publication authority are distinct:** a normal Finish authorizes draining already eligible saves and sealing only the reconciled immutable revision. The capture toggle is then OFF. Publication checks that closure authorization plus current enrollment/policy/revocation, not that the capture toggle remains ON. Expiry, restart or lost health grants no closure authority: explicit held-session recovery must resolve gaps/private exceptions before a corrected revision can be authorized. Finish is never permission to sweep all post-expiry camera files. Independently reconcile **all new stock-camera saves in that bounded interval** against both moved-file journal and files remaining in `DCIM/Camera`. This is private local inventory evidence, not content upload. A gap-free save ledger or equivalent baseline-plus-delta method must cover files removed/moved while running; event counts alone are circular. Access and reliability are **UNVERIFIED**. Late saves belonging to the interval and pending camera writes must resolve before sealing; uncertain races hold the run.

The closed inventory includes session/policy/version, start/end/expiry state, reconciled count, ordered occurrences, byte lengths, original/upload digests, separator positions and immutable closure revision. The Finish screen reports total captures (including every separator), moved/ready count, unmoved/unresolved count, book groups and interruptions. The person checks completed book/session counts without selecting files. Independent inventory detects lost captured files; it cannot prove every physical page was photographed. Use expected book/page counts where known and existing endpoint/content review. Without expected per-book counts, semantic page completeness remains unproved; never label transport integrity as that stronger result.

**OFF or killed automation must not create an apparently complete short book.** Mid-session OFF, expiry, reboot, heartbeat gap, missed save or unmatched file makes the entire affected session incomplete. Unmoved camera files are gaps, never automatically swept into research: personal shots may occupy that interval. Resolve only the bounded interval privately, retain every black separator and record approved recovered occurrences or explicit personal exclusions. Recovery can use F3's whole-session range operation without 200 individual selections. Freeze only the corrected complete revision. A fresh session starting halfway through a book cannot certify earlier pages: Start requires a known book boundary or explicit resumption of the held session. If Start was never used, the system cannot know shooting happened; no authorized manifest exists, and absence of the required ready indication must stop the photographer. Do not claim remote detection of an unannounced session.

`freeze_sources` checks expected staged positions against the supplied manifest; it cannot discover a photo omitted from it. `split_source_manifest` sees only supplied separators and accepts a trailing group: a missing separator, internal page or whole tail can look valid. Compare every black-frame occurrence/position against independent local inventory; never drop repeated black bytes as duplicates. Missing boundaries, ambiguous darkness, count discrepancies or a multi-book run with no separators block freeze/automatic binding; the no-separator single-group fallback cannot hide gaps. No new terminal black shot is required or presumed. Timeout/screen-off is a privacy pause, not completion.

After Finish, reconciliation and policy checks, publish the sanitized session and closed inventory. The server waits for every object and verifies bytes even if the closure marker arrives first. Feed **60/60/60/20 internal chunks without four selections**, through shared allocator/checkpoints, `stage_sources`, `freeze_sources`, splitter and existing confirmation/binding/jobs. Decision 013 still implements explicit completion and endpoint/title review. Later standing-policy automatic confirmation needs an explicit extension of that shared policy with exceptions held, never direct Book creation or a UI bypass. Until then existing review remains; local Finish does not replace server review. Supervised restart recovery is also new work: today's Streamlit threads do not survive process loss.
